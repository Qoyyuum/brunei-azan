Brunei Azan shows prayer times for Brunei Darussalam and sounds an azan (or beep) alarm at each prayer you enable.

Features:

* Today's prayer times with a live countdown to the next prayer
* Monthly schedule fetched from waktu.co.bn, cached on-device for offline use
* Per-prayer alarms (Subuh, Syuruk, Zohor, Asar, Maghrib, Isyak) plus your own custom alarms
* Alarms survive reboot and reschedule automatically when settings change
* Choice of alarm sounds, with preview
* Companion Wear OS app (watch app and watch face) and Android TV app

The F-Droid package is the "foss" build: it contains no Google Play Services, so phone↔watch settings sync is disabled — each device keeps its own settings. Everything else works fully offline once the month's prayer times are fetched.

Prayer times data: https://waktu.co.bn

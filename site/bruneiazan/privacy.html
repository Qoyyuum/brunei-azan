Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy applies to the Brunei Azan app for Android and Wear OS, published by Anak IT Brunei.

Data we collect

None. Brunei Azan does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

What leaves your device

The app makes one kind of network request: it asks waktu.co.bn for prayer times for your chosen district and date. The request contains only the district name and the date — no personal information, device identifiers, or location.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (district, theme, font, alarm choices and custom alarms) and cached prayer times are stored locally on your device only. When your watch is paired with your phone, these settings sync between the two devices via Google's Wearable Data Layer — the data stays between your own devices and is never sent to us.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to alert you when an azan alarm fires.
  • Exact alarms — to sound the azan at the correct prayer time.
  • Run at boot — to restore your alarms after a restart.
  • Vibration — optional, for alarm vibration.

The app does not request location access; the district is selected manually.

Third parties

The app embeds no third-party SDKs. Its only external dependency is the waktu.co.bn prayer-times service operated by Pusat Da'wah Islamiah, Kementerian Hal Ehwal Ugama.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
